Dibenzo[hi,st]ovalene (DBOV) is a nanographene molecule with strong red emission, high photostability, and optical gain — properties that make it attractive for lasers and strong light–matter coupling in microcavities. This study investigates how different peripheral chemical substituents affect the stimulated emission and nonlinear optical behavior of three DBOV derivatives using ultrafast spectroscopy. The results show that the substituent pattern modulates intermolecular interactions and thus the nonlinear response, offering a chemical handle to tune these nanographenes for photonic applications.

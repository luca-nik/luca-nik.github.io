Real-world plasmonic nanoparticles operate in solution, yet most atomistic models treat them in vacuum. This work presents ωFQFμ/FQ, a multiscale classical model that couples an atomistic description of the nanoparticle's plasmonic response with a polarizable force-field description of the surrounding solvent, making it possible to simulate real-size colloidal nanoparticles of generic chemical nature. Validated against reference quantum-mechanical calculations, the model accurately captures solvent-induced shifts of the plasmon resonance at a fraction of the computational cost.

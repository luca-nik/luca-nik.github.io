This paper introduces plasmonX, an open-source software package that implements the family of atomistic electromagnetic models (ωFQ, ωFQFμ and their multiscale extensions) developed for simulating plasmonic nanostructures. The code allows users to compute the optical response of metal and graphene-based nanosystems at the atomistic level, in both the frequency and time domains, and to couple them with quantum-mechanical descriptions of nearby molecules. It is designed to make state-of-the-art nanoplasmonics modeling accessible and reproducible for the wider community.

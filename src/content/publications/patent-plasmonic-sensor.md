This patent protects a fully computational workflow for designing plasmonic sensors with single-molecule sensitivity. Starting from a target analyte and a library of nanostructured plasmonic substrates, the method maps the analyte–substrate interaction potential at the atomistic level, computes the resulting spectroscopic signal and its enhancement factor, and compares it against a predefined sensitivity threshold. The result is a screening procedure that identifies promising sensor designs before any fabrication, reducing experimental trial and error.

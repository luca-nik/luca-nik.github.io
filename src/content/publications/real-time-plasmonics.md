Atomistic electromagnetic models of plasmonic nanoparticles are usually formulated in the frequency domain, which limits the study of time-resolved phenomena and ultrafast laser excitation. This paper reformulates the ωFQ and ωFQFμ models in the real-time domain, propagating the induced charges and dipoles of each atom as the system responds to an external pulse. The approach reproduces frequency-domain results while opening the way to simulating time-dependent plasmon dynamics, nonlinear regimes, and arbitrary excitation schemes in large nanostructures.

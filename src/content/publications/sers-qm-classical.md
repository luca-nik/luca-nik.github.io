Surface-enhanced Raman scattering (SERS) lets researchers detect molecules near plasmonic nanostructures, but simulating the effect requires treating both the molecule and the metal at high accuracy. This work combines a quantum-mechanical description of the adsorbed molecule with the atomistic classical models ωFQ and ωFQFμ for the plasmonic substrate, yielding a multiscale method that works for both noble-metal and graphene-based nanostructures. The approach captures the electromagnetic enhancement of Raman signals and provides an interpretable, computationally affordable tool for studying molecule–nanostructure interactions.
